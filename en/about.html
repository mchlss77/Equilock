Every mechanism runs automatically and doesn't depend on the human factor.

    • TRC-20 · ERC-20 · BEP-20

Technical architecture

Infrastructure you don't have to trust

The 2-of-3 key scheme guarantees that a payout needs the consent of two of the three parties. In a normal deal these are the buyer and the seller; in a dispute — the arbitrator and the party the decision favors. 

// EscrowVault.sol — payout condition
require(signatures.length >= 2);
require(!frozen || arbiterSigned);
release(recipient, amount);

  • Multisig 2-of-3 smart contracts

    Three keys: buyer, seller and arbitrator. Any movement of funds requires two signatures. No party, including the platform, can act alone.

  • No custodial risk

    The platform doesn't keep funds in its own wallets. Assets sit in the contract, and the arbitrator's key is stored in an HSM with split access.

  • Fast-Track arbitration

    For deals with clear evidence — priority review within 2 hours. An AI analyst prepares a conclusion, and a certified arbitrator signs the final decision. Arbitration rules
