FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Didn't find an answer? We reply 24/7.

Contact support
What is escrow and why is it needed?

Escrow is conditional holding of funds. The seller's USDT is locked in a smart contract and goes to the buyer only after payment is confirmed, so neither party can disappear with the money.

Who holds the keys to the multisig address?

The three keys belong to the buyer, the seller and the arbitrator. A payout needs two of the three signatures, so the platform can't manage the funds on its own.

What happens if the counterparty doesn't pay?

If payment doesn't arrive before the timer runs out, the deal is cancelled and the USDT is returned to the seller. Disputed cases are reviewed by arbitration.

How does the same-name rule work?

The payment must come from an account whose owner matches the verified deal participant. Transfers from other people's cards are rejected automatically.

Which networks are supported?

TRC-20, ERC-20 and BEP-20.
